## Overview
When severe monsoon floods strike, traditional rescue equipment often fails—boats are easily damaged by hidden underwater debris, and heavy trucks get permanently stuck in deep mud.

The <b>A.R.C. </b>(Amphibious Rescue Craft) is an AI-powered, all-terrain next-generation rescue vehicle engineered to solve this crisis. By merging heavy-duty amphibious rubber tracks with advanced edge computing, autonomous pathfinding, and a multi-stage extraction ecosystem, the<b>A.R.C. </b>safely navigates hazardous disaster zones to save lives when standard vehicles cannot.

## Key Features & Architecture
### Robust Amphibious Chassis: 
Features a reinforced impact-resistant armored hull and dual high-traction rubber tracks that provide seamless transit across land, mud, and deep water.

### Real-Time Edge AI Perception: 
Equipped with a front-facing Sensors Mast combining optical cameras, thermal heat vision, and mmWave radar. An onboard Edge NPU processes multi-spectral data in 10ms to detect human body heat through heavy rain and thick smoke.

 Autonomous Pathfinding: Machine-learning trajectory engine operating at 100 Hz to proactively navigate around shifting underwater wreckage and volatile currents.

 ### Integrated Aerial Recon Drone: 
 Features a weatherproof top docking deck with automated contact charging for a compact scout quadframe that performs high-altitude thermal mapping ahead of the craft.

 ### Rapid Rescue Mechanics:

### Telescopic Slide: 
Stern-mounted motorized slide (1.2m to 3.6m reach, 300kg capacity) to safely extract survivors from rooftops and steep elevation gaps.

### Target-Lock Float Launcher: 
Pneumatic life-ring launcher with AI wind and current calculation (30m range).

### Modular Storage: 
40L weather-sealed internal compartment for medical gear and emergency supplies.

## Future Scalability & Improvements
### Expanding Side Walls: 
Modular hydraulic panels designed to widen the hull footprint and safely scale capacity from 12 to 33 total occupants (30 survivors + 3 crew).

### AI Fleet Sync: 
Ad-hoc mesh network capabilities allowing multiple<b>A.R.C. </b>units to coordinate and automatically split up search grids across large regional disaster zones.

###Precision Robotic Arm: 
Deck-mounted automated arm to clear heavy debris, retrieve floating objects, or deliver supplies directly to survivors.
