# Amphibious-Rescue-Craft (A.R.C)

Next-Generation Amphibious Search, Rescue & Command Platform



# Overview
When severe monsoon floods strike, traditional rescue equipment often fails—boats are easily damaged by hidden underwater debris, and heavy trucks get permanently stuck in deep mud[cite: 1].

The A.R.C. (Amphibious Rescue Craft) is an AI-powered, all-terrain next-generation rescue vehicle engineered to solve this crisis[cite: 1]. By merging heavy-duty amphibious rubber tracks with advanced edge computing, autonomous pathfinding, and a multi-stage extraction ecosystem, the A.R.C. safely navigates hazardous disaster zones to save lives when standard vehicles cannot[cite: 1].

# Key Features & Architecture
 Robust Amphibious Chassis: Features a reinforced impact-resistant armored hull and dual high-traction rubber tracks that provide seamless transit across land, mud, and deep water.

 Real-Time Edge AI Perception: Equipped with a front-facing Sensors Mast combining optical cameras, thermal heat vision, and mmWave radar[cite: 1]. An onboard Edge NPU processes multi-spectral data in 10ms to detect human body heat through heavy rain and thick smoke[cite: 1].

 Autonomous Pathfinding: Machine-learning trajectory engine operating at 100 Hz to proactively navigate around shifting underwater wreckage and volatile currents[cite: 1].

 Integrated Aerial Recon Drone: Features a weatherproof top docking deck with automated contact charging for a compact scout quadframe that performs high-altitude thermal mapping ahead of the craft[cite: 1].

 Rapid Rescue Mechanics:

Telescopic Slide: Stern-mounted motorized slide (1.2m to 3.6m reach, 300kg capacity) to safely extract survivors from rooftops and steep elevation gaps[cite: 1].

Target-Lock Float Launcher: Pneumatic life-ring launcher with AI wind and current calculation (30m range)[cite: 1].

Modular Storage: 40L weather-sealed internal compartment for medical gear and emergency supplies[cite: 1].

# Future Scalability & Improvements
Expanding Side Walls: Modular hydraulic panels designed to widen the hull footprint and safely scale capacity from 12 to 33 total occupants (30 survivors + 3 crew)[cite: 1].

AI Fleet Sync: Ad-hoc mesh network capabilities allowing multiple A.R.C. units to coordinate and automatically split up search grids across large regional disaster zones.

Precision Robotic Arm: Deck-mounted automated arm to clear heavy debris, retrieve floating objects, or deliver supplies directly to survivors[cite: 1].
